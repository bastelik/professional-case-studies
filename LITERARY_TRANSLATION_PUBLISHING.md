# Case Study: Literary Translation & Cross-Cultural Publishing

## Context

Independent literary translation and editorial work has connected scholarship, cultural criticism and contemporary humanities writing with Brazilian readers. The work has involved authors writing in French, English, German, Dutch and Spanish, and publication with Brazilian publishing houses.

## Contribution

The professional practice includes book-length translation, editorial collaboration and the careful transfer of concepts, argument, voice and cultural context across languages. Published work includes authors such as Achille Mbembe, Frantz Fanon, Aimé Césaire, Édouard Glissant, Felwine Sarr and Anton de Kom.

The work has been supported at different times by international literary and cultural institutions, including Dutch, Flemish, French and German funding bodies.

## Operating challenges

- Preserving conceptual precision and authorial voice
- Researching historically and culturally specific references
- Maintaining consistency across long-form manuscripts
- Collaborating with editors and publishers through revision
- Balancing readability with fidelity to complex arguments

## Transferable capabilities

- Advanced multilingual research and writing
- Terminology and concept management
- Long-form quality assurance
- Editorial project coordination
- Cross-cultural communication
- Sustained delivery against publication requirements
