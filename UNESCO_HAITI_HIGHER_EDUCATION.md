# Case Study: Higher Education Coordination in Post-Earthquake Haiti

## Context

Following the 2010 earthquake in Haiti, professional work connected to a UNESCO higher education mission addressed the challenges facing the higher education sector in a period of institutional disruption and recovery.

Related work also included assessment activities concerning Haitian refugees in an academic context at the University of Campinas (UNICAMP). These are distinct activities and should not be conflated into a single project.

## Contribution

The professional experience involved international coordination, research and assessment in higher education and migration-related contexts. The work required communication across institutional and linguistic boundaries and sensitivity to the circumstances of affected communities.

A related multilingual higher-education publication on Haiti was developed in Portuguese, English, French, Spanish and Haitian Creole, involving collaboration across academic and institutional contexts.

## Operating challenges

- Coordinating information across institutions and disciplines
- Working in a post-disaster environment with changing needs
- Communicating across multiple languages and educational contexts
- Maintaining sensitivity to institutional constraints and affected populations
- Translating complex findings into usable documentation

## Transferable capabilities

- International program and stakeholder coordination
- Research and qualitative assessment
- Multilingual documentation and editorial coordination
- Higher education and institutional cooperation
- Cross-cultural communication
- Knowledge transfer in complex settings
