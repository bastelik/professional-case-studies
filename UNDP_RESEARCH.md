# Case Study: International Development Research & Conflict Analysis

## Context

Professional work for the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) included research and analytical work relating to armed groups in Haiti. The work required engagement with a complex political and security context and the careful handling of information across institutional and linguistic settings.

## Contribution

The professional contribution included research, analysis and reporting on armed groups in Haiti in 2006. This work required attention to source quality, context, terminology and the distinction between reported information and analytical interpretation.

## Operating challenges

- Working with information produced by different actors and for different purposes
- Assessing claims in a politically and operationally sensitive environment
- Maintaining clarity when concepts do not map neatly across languages
- Producing structured analysis for an institutional audience
- Avoiding overstatement where evidence or source access is limited

## Transferable capabilities

- Research planning and source assessment
- Analytical writing and synthesis
- Cross-cultural and multilingual communication
- Context-sensitive stakeholder engagement
- Evidence qualification and documentation
- Work in complex international environments
